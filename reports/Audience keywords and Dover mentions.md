# Lead with home services, demote Dover

No single audience word wins on every platform, so Zonic should give each word a job rather than crown one. "Small business" has the most searchers everywhere (about **136,000 B2B searches a month** on Google by Semrush's count, 4–6 times the Google Trends interest of the contractor and home-service phrases, and the only one of the four with YouTube demand or Bing data), but it is the most contested word (median keyword difficulty 42), and Zonic's homepage, titled for it, ranks for none of its keywords. Among the trade words, "contractor(s)" has the most B2B demand (**39,000 a month, against 27,000 for "home service(s)" and 17,000 for "local business"**) and Zonic's largest Search Console footprint, but 93% of that footprint is trade-prefixed ("painting contractor marketing"), and the word on its own belongs to homeowners. "Home service(s)" is the right umbrella for the trades: it has the lowest difficulty (median KD 17), leads agency-shopping and digital-marketing searches, is the name Google, the field-service software makers and the leading niche agencies already use, covers every Zonic trade from roofing to pest control, earns Zonic's best average position (30.5), and was the only one of four ChatGPT prompts that named Zonic, while "local business" trails almost everywhere and belongs only inside service names such as "local SEO". On Dover the owner is right in substance: Dover queries produced **1,865 impressions and zero clicks in three months**, and the Google Business Profile link that ranks near #1 for those searches has no Dover sentence in its copy, so the hero's "Our Dover, Delaware team" and the street address in service-page contact blocks can go. Keeping the address only in the footer and on the contact page goes too far, though: keep Dover in full on the four Delaware and Wilmington pages, keep one factual "headquartered in Dover, serving businesses across the U.S." sentence on About, in the homepage FAQ and on each city page, and say "across the U.S." rather than "worldwide" unless non-US clients can be shown. The bigger risk is truthfulness, not traffic: 8 The Green is a registered-agent building that rents virtual offices, so every "visit our Dover office" claim must be literally true, and the Business Profile's eligibility depends on Suite B being a staffed office.

## Small business wins every volume count and almost none of the reachable demand

"Small business" leads every raw measure of demand. Semrush counts **136,080 US B2B searches a month** across 65 small-business marketing, SEO, website and advertising phrases (106,820 after merging likely duplicate variants), against 39,040 for contractor(s), 26,760 for home service(s) and 16,600 for local business, with consumer searches excluded from all four ([Semrush: small business marketing](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/?db=us&q=small+business+marketing); [contractor marketing](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/?db=us&q=contractor+marketing); [home service marketing](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/?db=us&q=home+service+marketing); [local business marketing](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/?db=us&q=local+business+marketing)). On Google Trends' five-year US averages, "small business marketing" scores 22 against 4–6 for the other three, and "small business seo" 18 against 3–10 ([Google Trends: marketing wording](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=US&q=contractor%20marketing,home%20service%20marketing,local%20business%20marketing,small%20business%20marketing); [SEO wording](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=US&q=contractor%20seo,home%20service%20seo,local%20business%20seo,small%20business%20seo)). On YouTube only the small-business phrase has steady search interest (five-year average 34), while the contractor and home-service phrases registered in just 7 and 5 of 262 weeks ([Google Trends: YouTube search](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=US&q=contractor%20marketing,home%20service%20marketing,local%20business%20marketing,small%20business%20marketing&gprop=youtube)). ## Contractor demand is real but split between homeowners and trade names

The owner's sense that "contractors" is the bigger word is right, mostly because homeowners use it. Four hiring searches alone, "general contractor" (49,500 a month), "contractors near me" (33,100), "general contractors near me" (22,200) and "contractor reviews" (320), total **105,120 searches a month, 2.7 times all B2B contractor-marketing demand** ([Semrush: general contractor](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us&q=general+contractor); [contractors near me](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us&q=contractors+near+me); [general contractors near me](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us&q=general+contractors+near+me)). Bing shows the same skew, with thousands of "contractors near me" impressions while "contractor marketing" stays below its reporting threshold ([Bing: contractor marketing](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/keywordresearch?siteUrl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.zonicllc.com%2F&keyword=contractor+marketing)). None of that homeowner traffic reaches Zonic today: 523 of the 527 contractor queries in its Search Console are marketing searches, and homeowner queries total five impressions ([GSC: contractor](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=query&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&query=*contractor)). Keeping it that way means using "contractor" only inside marketing phrases; a heading such as "Contractors in Tampa" reads as a homeowner looking to hire.

Within B2B demand, contractor phrasing leads home service by about 1.5 times and local business by about 2.4 times, and a separate pull of roughly 150 phrases produced nearly the same ratios, 1.4 and 2.7 ([Semrush: contractor seo](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/?db=us&q=contractor+seo); [contractor website](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/?db=us&q=contractor+website)). The lead depends on the service. Contractor wording wins **SEO (12,020 searches a month against 7,510 for home service)** and **website design (8,150 against 700)**, plus ads (2,260 against 1,420) and leads (2,270 against 1,150), while home-service wording wins digital marketing (6,250 against 2,900) and ties on agency shopping (4,400 against 4,380). The best contractor targets are cheap to rank for: "contractor seo" and "seo for contractors" draw 2,900 searches each at KD 19 and 21, and because Semrush counts them as separate keywords a page needs both word orders; "contractor website design" adds 1,900 at KD 22, "contractor web design" 1,000 at KD 12 and "ppc for contractors" 590 at KD 2 ([contractor web design](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us&q=contractor+web+design); [ppc for contractors](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us&q=ppc+for+contractors)). Of the eight results pages checked, the three contractor-worded ones were the only ones with ads at the top, a sign that advertisers pay for these clicks ([Semrush: seo for contractors](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us&q=seo+for+contractors)).

On zonicllc.com the word already brings **16,157 impressions in three months, the most of the four, but 93% come from trade-prefixed searches**: painting (3,412), electrical (2,867), window and door (2,106), roofing (2,025) and flooring (1,167) lead, while queries with no trade in front of "contractor" add only 1,169 impressions and no clicks ([GSC: contractor](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=query&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&query=*contractor)). Semrush finds Zonic at #5–#28 on trade pages but ranking for no generic contractor keyword above 300 searches a month, because no generic contractor page exists ([Semrush: zonicllc.com](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/positions/?db=us&q=zonicllc.com)). Elsewhere, one dedicated page is what wins: Blue Corona's /digital-marketing/contractors/ ranks #1 for "contractor marketing agency" (1,600), "contractor digital marketing" (1,000), "contractor marketing company" (590) and "marketing agency for contractors" (590) ([Semrush: bluecorona.com](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/positions/?db=us&q=bluecorona.com)). The broad "contractor marketing" searches reward guides, with Reddit, Footbridge's "2026 contractor marketing tips" and Nextdoor's "10 marketing tips" near the top, so such a page needs a real how-to section, not only a pitch ([Semrush: contractor marketing results](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us&q=contractor+marketing)).

"Contractor" cannot be Zonic's umbrella word, because about half its trades are not contractors. US industry codes file HVAC, plumbing, electrical and roofing under Specialty Trade Contractors in the Construction sector, but landscaping, pest control and janitorial work under Services to Buildings and Dwellings, and home inspection under Building Inspection Services ([NAICS 238220](https://www.naics.com/naics-code-description/?code=238220); [BLS: NAICS 238](https://www.bls.gov/iag/tgs/iag238.htm); [NAICS 561730](https://www.naics.com/naics-code-description/?code=561730); [NAICS 541350](https://www.naics.com/naics-code-description/?code=541350)). Trade associations split the same way, with ACCA and NRCA calling their members contractors and NALP not, and owners name their trade first: the Reddit thread "Need help marketing a pest control company" ranks #1 for "pest control marketing" ([ACCA](https://www.acca.org/); [NALP](https://www.landscapeprofessionals.org/); [r/sweatystartup](https://www.reddit.com/r/sweatystartup/comments/y3evxd/need_help_marketing_a_pest_control_company/)). Seventeen trade phrases such as "hvac marketing" (2,900), "seo for plumbers" (2,900) and "roofing marketing agency" (2,400) add up to about **27,900 searches a month at KD 3–38**, as much as either umbrella word, which is why the per-trade pages remain Zonic's main audience lever ([Semrush Keyword Overview](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us)).

## Home services is the category's own name and Zonic's clearest opening

"Home services" is what the category calls itself. Google's Local Services Ads began with home services, list "General contractor" as one of about 37 services in the Home group, and let "Home service providers" earn a Google Guarantee badge ([Google Local Services Ads](https://business.google.com/us/ad-solutions/local-service-ads/); [Google blog, 2023](https://blog.google/outreach-initiatives/small-business/local-services-ads-expands/)), and Microsoft lists "home services" as a Professional Service Ads vertical ([Microsoft Advertising](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/april-2023/professional-service-ads-and-other-product-updates-for-april)). Housecall Pro sells "Home Services Business Management Software", Workiz targets "home service pros", Jobber's flagship research is its "Home Service Economic Report", and Angi recruits "200,000+ home service pros"; ServiceTitan is the outlier, branding on "the trades" ([Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/); [Workiz](https://www.workiz.com/); [Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-reports); [Angi for Pros](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id437003175); [ServiceTitan](https://www.servicetitan.com/)). The niche agencies that rank and get recommended, among them Hook Agency, Blue Corona, Scorpion, Rival Digital, WebFX's industry hub, Geek Powered and homeservices.marketing, put "home services" in their homepage titles and catch contractor searches with a separate contractor page ([Hook Agency](https://hookagency.com/); [Blue Corona](https://www.bluecorona.com/); [Scorpion](https://www.scorpion.co/home-services/)).

It is also the easiest of the four to rank with. Home-service phrases have the **lowest median keyword difficulty (17)**, and 14 of them with at least 300 searches a month sit at KD 20 or below, 10,500 searches in all, more low-difficulty volume than the generic small-business (8,280) or contractor (8,080) phrases offer ([Semrush: home service marketing](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/?db=us&q=home+service+marketing); [home service seo](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordmagic/?db=us&q=home+service+seo)). They lead the phrasings of someone shopping for an agency: "home services marketing agency" (1,900, KD 17), "home services digital marketing" (1,300, KD 16), "home services digital marketing agency" (720, KD 14) and "home service marketing company" (480, KD 9). The results let small sites in: homeservices.marketing, with an Authority Score of 17 and 330 referring domains, holds #2 for "home service marketing agency" and #5 for "home service marketing", a band that Zonic, at an Authority Score in the high single digits, sits just below ([Semrush: home service marketing agency results](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us&q=home+service+marketing+agency); [homeservices.marketing backlinks](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/backlinks/overview/?q=homeservices.marketing&searchType=root_domain)).

Zonic's own data points the same way. "Home service" queries drew **6,238 impressions across 233 queries at an average position of 30.5, the best of the four**; all 233 are B2B, 78% are SEO-related, and the plural "home services" carries two-thirds of the impressions ([GSC: home service](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=query&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&query=*home%20service)). Semrush shows 15 home-service variants at #15–#38, all on /services/local-seo-for-home-services, with "home services seo" (2,400 a month) at #39, the nearest high-volume target on the site ([Semrush: zonicllc.com](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/positions/?db=us&q=zonicllc.com)). It was also the only one of four "best agency" prompts in which ChatGPT named Zonic, calling it "A Delaware-based digital marketing agency serving local and home service businesses across the US" and its pick for "Affordable local SEO and GBP management", with Zonic's own homepage as the citation ([ChatGPT prompt 2](https://chatgpt.com/c/6b07f0b8-4b54-83e8-9b88-46bd29fffaf2)). The description was accurate down to the price, since "$197/month with annual billing" is the Foundation plan's annual rate, which shows ChatGPT reads and repeats Zonic's own published figures; it was a single run in the owner's logged-in account, which holds a Zonic project folder, so personalization cannot be ruled out. ChatGPT also treated contractor and home service as one category, naming six of the same firms in both answers, and local and small business as another, with seven firms shared, so Zonic surfaced only in the trades category. Yet only 2 of 212 routes use "home service(s)" in a title, H1 or meta description.

Two limits keep "home services" off the homepage headline. Zonic also markets dental practices, law firms, chiropractors, real-estate agents, cannabis dispensaries, travel firms and non-profits, none of which are home services, so the homepage and all-industry service pages need a broader word, which is the one job "small business" does well. And the bare noun pulls in consumers: "home services" alone draws 8,100 searches a month, and brand searches such as "cinch home services" (9,900 a month, a home warranty) share the phrase ([Semrush: home services](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/keywordoverview/?db=us&q=home+services); [Semrush: reddit.com rankings](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/organic/positions/?db=us&q=reddit.com)).

## Dover copy earns almost nothing outside the Delaware pages

The traffic at stake is small and sits on a few URLs. In three months, queries containing "dover" produced **54 queries, 1,865 impressions (0.35% of the site) and no clicks**, and queries containing "delaware" produced 17,261 impressions (3.25%) and **9 clicks** ([GSC: dover](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=query&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&query=*dover); [GSC: delaware](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=query&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&query=*delaware)). The Delaware and Wilmington pages hold the organic share: /services/delaware/digital-marketing drew 6,348 Delaware-query impressions and 2 clicks, /services/delaware/web-design 5,346 and 1 click, and /services/delaware/seo 4,024 plus 616 Dover-query impressions ([GSC: delaware by page](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=page&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&query=*delaware); [GSC: dover by page](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=page&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&query=*dover)). The rest comes through the Google Business Profile. The non-www /services/local-seo-for-home-services URL and its gmb_zonic-tagged twin earned **73 clicks, 13.8% of all site clicks**, rank at or near #1 for "seo company dover", "seo services dover de" and "seo delaware", and took 6 of the 9 Delaware clicks, yet the page has no Dover or Delaware sentence in its copy; its only Dover text sits in its contact block and the sitewide footer ([GSC: Business Profile URL](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=query&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&page=!https%3A%2F%2Fzonicllc.com%2Fservices%2Flocal-seo-for-home-services); [GSC: gmb_zonic URLs](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=query&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&page=*gmb_zonic); [page.tsx](file:///Users/niteshmaan/Desktop/zonic-media/src/app/%28main%29/services/local-seo-for-home-services/page.tsx)). The older templates ran an accidental test. Until the late-October redesign, 13 niche marketing-agency pages repeated Dover in an FAQ answer ("We're based in Dover, Delaware"), a trust chip and the footer; that legacy copy now sits unrendered in a data file and in the 17 October text snapshots ([industryMarketingPages.generated.json](file:///Users/niteshmaan/Desktop/zonic-media/src/data/industryMarketingPages.generated.json)). Over three months the 13 URLs drew a combined **30 Dover-query and 87 Delaware-query impressions, and no clicks**, and the only measurable ranking among them is the auto-repair page at about #4 for "dover auto repair seo service", with 29 impressions ([GSC: dover by page](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=page&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&query=*dover)). Another 1,232 Dover impressions came from queries with no state, which Search Console cannot split between Dover, Delaware and the Dovers in New Hampshire and New York that also appear in the data.

This matches how Google says ranking works. Nothing in its helpful-content guidance, spam policies or September 2025 rater guidelines asks for an address or headquarters city in body copy; raters are told that "Most websites have 'contact us' or 'about us' or 'about' pages that provide information about who owns the site" ([Search Quality Rater Guidelines, Sep 2025](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/guidelines.raterhub.com/en//searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf); [Google: helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)). Local results turn on a Business Profile's relevance, distance and prominence, and the top local-pack factors in Whitespark's 2026 survey are all profile signals: primary category, proximity to the searcher, keywords in the business name and an address in the city searched ([Google: how local ranking works](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091); [Whitespark 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)). City keywords in content are Whitespark's second-strongest local organic factor, but they add relevance only for the city named, and Google localizes searches such as "SEO company" to the searcher, so a Dover line on a roofing page helps only Dover searches the Delaware pages already serve ([Google: ranking results](https://www.google.com/search/howsearchworks/how-search-works/ranking-results/)). Nine Dover mentions on one homepage also drift toward the spam policy's "Repeating the same words or phrases so often that it sounds unnatural" ([Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)).

The case for one honest line is trust and identity, not rankings. Buyers do want to know where a vendor is: NN/g's About-page research found participants wanted to learn "where companies were located", and 44% of B2B buyers in KoMarketing's 2015 survey said they would leave a vendor site with no contact information or phone number ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/about-us-information-on-websites/); [KoMarketing 2015](https://webcf.waybackmachine.org/web/20211007154524/https://komarketing.com/files/b2b-web-usability-report-2015.pdf)). A footer address, a full contact page and one About line meet that need, and phone and email in every form block matter more than the street line. National agencies use a single sentence, such as EverConvert's "Headquartered in Greenville, South Carolina, we serve law firms and health care providers across the United States" ([EverConvert](https://www.everconvert.com/)). AI assistants read the site too: business websites supplied 42–44% of AI citations in two large studies, though Clutch is ChatGPT's top single source for agency prompts at 7%, and ChatGPT can use an approximate IP-based location for local results ([Yext](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251009106549/en/Yext-Research-86-of-AI-Citations-Come-from-Brand-Managed-Sources-Clarifying-How-Marketers-Can-Compete-in-the-AI-Search-Era); [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/); [Temso](https://www.temso.ai/data/AI-Source-Citation-Patterns-in-Marketing-and-Advertising-Agencies); [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897)). Deleting Dover will not make assistants see Zonic as national while Clutch and the Business Profile still say Dover; replacing it with "headquartered in Dover, Delaware, working with businesses across the U.S." gives them the right sentence to repeat.

## Truthful claims and a staged rollout matter more than the deletions

The research turned up a bigger issue than where the address appears. **8 The Green, Dover, is owned by A Registered Agent, Inc.**, which sells "a local business address with a unique suite number" and $19-a-month virtual offices in the building and tells customers they can list the address on their websites; Nielsen Norman Group, a distributed firm, lists a suite there as its "Mailing Address" ([Delaware Registered Agent](https://www.delawareregisteredagent.com/?p=868); [NN/g contact page](https://www.nngroup.com/about/contact/)). Google's Business Profile rules say a virtual office is not eligible and that service-area businesses "can't list a 'virtual' office unless that office is staffed during business hours" ([Google Business Profile guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)). Only the owner knows whether Suite B is a staffed office. If it is, the Delaware pages can keep saying so. If it is not, the riskiest text on the site is not the hero line but the claims that a visitor can walk in, such as "you can drop by our Dover office", "an SEO company Delaware owners can visit" and "our Dover office is pinned on Google Maps", which Google's rater guidelines would rate Low as "exaggerated or mildly misleading", or Lowest if deceptive ([Search Quality Rater Guidelines, p.63](https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/guidelines.raterhub.com/en//searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf)). Either way, the Business Profile behind 13.8% of the site's clicks carries this exposure whatever the body copy says, and hiding its address is no quick fix: in a small 2023 Sterling Sky test, hiding a client's address made its local-pack rankings drop "almost completely" until the address was restored ([Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-business-profile-address-local-rankings-35570.html)).

"Worldwide" adds risk without adding reach. Google infers a site's target country from structural signals, including country domains, hreflang, "local addresses and phone numbers on the pages", currency, links from local sites and the Business Profile, and it retired Search Console's country-targeting setting in 2022 ([Google: multi-regional sites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites); [Search Console Help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/12474899?hl=en)). Every Zonic signal already says US: USD plans, a US phone number, US city pages and US schema. An unsupported global claim invites the rater guidelines' Low rating for exaggeration and hands AI tools a vague "global agency" label that conflicts with Clutch and the Business Profile. Some non-US interest is real, since 57% of the site's 530 clicks came from outside the US even though 91% of impressions were American, but clicks are not clients ([GSC: US view](https://search.google.com/search-console/performance/search-analytics?resource_id=sc-domain%3Azonicllc.com&breakdown=query&num_of_months=3&metrics=CLICKS%2CIMPRESSIONS%2CPOSITION&country=usa)). Use "across the U.S." in heroes, metadata and schema (`areaServed: United States`), use "all 50 states", as the homepage does today, only if there are clients in every state, and add one About or FAQ line about non-US clients only if they can be named.

The edits carry their own mechanical risks. Removing the address tile cuts visible word count on about 165 routes, which trips keyword-guard's "word count dropped" check until the baseline is refreshed, and title, description, H1 and H2 changes are flagged by design; no saved Search Console query contains "dover", so the query check passes, but "delaware" must stay in the Delaware pages' text ([keyword-guard.mjs](file:///Users/niteshmaan/Desktop/zonic-media/scripts/keyword-guard.mjs); [saved query files](file:///Users/niteshmaan/Desktop/zonic-media/reports/gsc/queries/)). Eight components use a contact grid whose last tile spans two columns, so each needs a visual check once a tile goes, and `SITE_CONTACT.address` also feeds the footer and lead emails, so the tile should be dropped in each template rather than the constant edited ([siteConfig.ts](file:///Users/niteshmaan/Desktop/zonic-media/src/shared/siteConfig.ts)). Under the project's sitemap-lastmod rule, FAQ rewrites and any About title change count as major edits, so the owner decides those dates, and llms.txt mirrors About's metadata and says "all work done in-house by the Dover team", so it needs the same truth check ([llms.txt](file:///Users/niteshmaan/Desktop/zonic-media/public/llms.txt)). The Business Profile landing page should be exempt from all of it.

## Conclusion

Both questions come down to the same thing: the words on a page decide which searches Zonic can compete for, but other sites decide whether it gets chosen. Sites with Authority Scores of 10–20 already hold top-ten spots for home-service, contractor and local-business marketing searches ([Semrush: seoforcontractors.agency](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/backlinks/overview/?q=seoforcontractors.agency&searchType=root_domain); [marylandlocalmarketing.com](https://www.semrush.com/analytics/backlinks/overview/?q=marylandlocalmarketing.com&searchType=root_domain)), and ChatGPT assembled its trades answers mainly from "best agency" roundups such as AgencyRanked's and Built-Right Digital's lists, alongside the agencies' own pages, and its generalist answers from Clutch rankings ([ChatGPT prompt 1](https://chatgpt.com/c/6b07f017-5530-83ee-a995-29b8e8e776fe); [prompt 4](https://chatgpt.com/c/6b07f14d-a240-83ee-8833-d52d4fe65de3)). Presence on expert-curated "best of" lists is also the top AI-visibility factor in Whitespark's 2026 survey ([Whitespark 2026](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)). The work most likely to lift Zonic for "home service marketing agency" or "contractor marketing agency", and to change how assistants describe its reach, is earning places in those roundups and stating national service lines and locations served on Clutch. The wording fix is cheap and mostly additive; the authority gap is the real limit.

The Dover question, meanwhile, is not really an SEO question. The measured traffic at stake is close to zero either way, while the truth of the office claims and the eligibility of the Business Profile bear on the URL that brings in about one click in seven. A cleanup that swaps "our Dover team" for an accurate headquarters sentence, rather than erasing Dover, tells Google, buyers and AI tools the same story, headquartered in Dover and working across the U.S., and that consistency is worth more than any single deletion.
