Local factor 0 1 Relevance How closely your Business Profile matches what the customer is searching for. Local factor 0 2 Distance How far the business is from the searcher, or from the location included in the search. Local visibility naturally changes as the search point changes. Local factor 0 3 Prominence How established and well known the business appears. Reviews, links, references across the web, and organic visibility can all contribute to local prominence. Why does my ranking look different when I search on my own phone? Because you are searching from your own location, usually your own address, and Google orders Map Pack results heavily by distance from the searcher. Your search history plays a part too. A rank checker runs the same search from many points around your area, which is how customers actually see you. Will checking my ranking affect my Google Business Profile? No. A rank checker only reads public search results. It doesn’t log in to your profile, change anything on it, or send any signal Google uses for ranking. How often should I check my Google Maps ranking? Once a month is enough for most local businesses, plus a check a few weeks after any real change, such as a new primary category, a review push or a moved address. Rankings move a little every day, so checking daily mostly shows you noise. What is a good Google Maps ranking? The top three, the Map Pack, is where most calls come from, so the goal is to be in the top three across the core of your service area, not only at your address. If your average across the grid is in single figures and the green covers the neighbourhoods you want work from, you’re in good shape. Is this the same as a Google My Business rank checker? Yes. What is the difference between a rank checker and a rank tracker? A rank checker gives you a snapshot of where you rank right now for a keyword. A rank tracker repeats the same scan on a schedule and stores the history, so you can see whether rankings are moving and which competitors are gaining. Why doesn’t my business show up on Google Maps at all? The most common causes are a profile that was never verified, a suspension, a primary category that doesn’t match the search, or a keyword your profile and website don’t mention. Check that the profile is live in Google Maps first; if it is suspended, it needs reinstating before any ranking work will help.
