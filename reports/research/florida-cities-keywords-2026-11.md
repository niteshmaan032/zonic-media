## Cannibalization rules

- City pages never use "florida digital marketing agency" or "south florida digital marketing agency" as head terms; those belong to the hub.
- City SEO, web design and PPC terms are secondary on the city page: an H3, an FAQ or body copy. No city-level SEO or web design page exists yet. If one is built later, these terms move to it.
- Tampa owns "Tampa Bay" and St. Petersburg owns "St. Pete" and "Pinellas", so their H1s and leads never overlap.
- Some Semrush terms contain competitor brand names (filtered out). Never use them.
