## How to evaluate any Delaware SEO company (the checklist)

1. **Ask for Delaware proof.** Rankings they hold for their own name + "Delaware"
   are the cheapest honesty test in the industry.
2. **Demand deliverable lists, not hour counts.** You should know what happens
   each month before you pay for it.
3. **Check the review trail** — Clutch and Google reviews from named local
   businesses beat testimonials on the agency's own site.
4. **Make them report business outcomes.** Calls, form fills and booked jobs;
   rankings alone are how bad agencies hide.
5. **Refuse long lock-ins.** A confident agency sells month-to-month.

**Q: How much does SEO cost in Delaware?**
A: Local campaigns typically run a few hundred to a few thousand dollars per
month depending on market competition. Wilmington costs more than Kent or
Sussex County. Get quotes scoped to your market, not a national rate card.

**Q: Should I hire a Delaware agency or a national one?**
A: For local search — Map Pack, "near me", town-level rankings — a Delaware
agency's local signals and market knowledge usually win. For national
e-commerce or enterprise content programs, a specialist national firm can fit.

**Q: How long does SEO take in Delaware?**
A: Google Business Profile improvements often move in 4–8 weeks; competitive
organic terms take 3–6 months. Distrust anyone promising faster.
