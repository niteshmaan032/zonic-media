## What this service does

An unverified Google Business Profile is invisible on Google Maps — and since Google's AI Overviews pull only from verified, active profiles, unverified businesses are also excluded from AI-generated local answers. Zonic Media:

1. Runs a **free verification audit** to identify why the profile won't verify and which verification methods are actually available for it.
2. Explains which verification methods Google offers the profile. Google picks the methods from business type, public info, region and hours, and they can't be changed on request: phone or text, email, live video call, video recording, postcard, and in some cases instant (Google Search Console) or bulk (10+ locations).
3. Prepares businesses for **video verification**: one unedited recording of at least 30 seconds, made live on a phone, showing the location (street signs or building numbers), the business name on permanent signage matching the profile, and proof of management (register, storage room, point-of-sale; for service-area businesses tools, a branded vehicle, or a permit, invoice or utility bill). No faces or private documents. Google reviews can take up to 5 business days; after a rejection, use "Review issues" and re-record including everything plus the missing proof.
4. Prepares owners for **live video call verification** — a real Google agent walks through the location in real time; this method requires specific preparation most guides don't cover.
5. Diagnoses and fixes **verification loops** and **revoked verifications**, which usually trace back to duplicate listings, policy violations, or inconsistent business data.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does verification take?**
Phone, text and email codes work within minutes; Google says video reviews can take up to 5 business days; postcard codes are valid for 30 days and a new request cancels the old code. Most of Zonic Media's cases are verified within 5–7 business days.

**Is Google Business Profile verification free?**
Google never charges for verification. Zonic Media charges nothing for its verification work if the profile isn't verified.

**Can a service-area business get verified?**
Yes — SABs can be verified without displaying a physical address; Zonic Media specializes in SAB verification.

**Why did my verification get revoked?**
Usually a deeper compliance issue: duplicate listing conflict, policy violation, or inconsistent business data. Fixing the root cause is what makes verification stick.
