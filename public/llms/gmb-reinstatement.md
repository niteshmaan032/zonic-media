## What this service does

When Google suspends a Business Profile, the listing disappears from Google Maps and local search, and the business immediately loses calls and customers. Zonic Media's reinstatement specialists:

1. Run a **free suspension audit** to diagnose the actual cause (policy violation, address issue, duplicate listing, category conflict, suspicious edits, or verification failure).
2. Fix the underlying compliance issues before appealing — the step most DIY appeals skip, which is why they fail.
3. File a documented reinstatement appeal with Google within 24–48 hours, with supporting evidence (business registration, utility bills, signage photos).
4. Follow up with Google until the profile is live, and resubmit with stronger documentation if an appeal is rejected.
5. Optimize the reinstated profile (categories, services, photos, posts) so it returns stronger and the suspension is less likely to recur.

**How long does Google Business Profile reinstatement take?**
Most cases resolve in 5–7 business days; complex cases needing video verification can take up to two weeks.

**Should I create a new profile instead?**
No. Google links new listings to the suspended one via address, phone, and account ownership, and usually suspends the new profile too — making the original case harder to win. Reinstating the original profile is the only safe path.

**What does it cost?**
The audit is free. A flat reinstatement fee is quoted after the audit, backed by the No Fix, No Charge policy.

**What documents are needed?**
